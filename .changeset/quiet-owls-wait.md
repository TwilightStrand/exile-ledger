#### Watchers now use at most half of the trade site's rate limits, leaving the rest for your own searches, and remember the site's counts when the browser restarts the extension's background, so background checks are much less likely to get you timed out on the trade site. Sync now stops, instead of overwriting, when another browser syncs with a newer version of Exile Ledger, and asks you to update.
